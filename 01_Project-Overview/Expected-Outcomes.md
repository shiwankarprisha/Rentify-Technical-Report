# Expected Outcomes

The expected outcomes of Rentify include:

1. A unified rental assistance platform for renters.

2. A structured property verification workflow involving vendors, administrators and verification partners.

3. Clear verification states for rental listings.

4. Better visibility into property, locality and credibility-related information.

5. Accessible rental guidance covering scams, tenancy rights and housing schemes.

6. A role-based workflow supporting different participants in the rental process.

7. An interactive prototype demonstrating the proposed system and its major workflows.

8. A foundation for future development of advanced recommendation, document verification and fraud-detection capabilities.
