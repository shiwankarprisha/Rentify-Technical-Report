# Objectives

The primary objectives of Rentify are:

1. To provide a unified platform for rental property discovery and assistance.

2. To introduce a structured workflow for property and ownership verification.

3. To help renters identify potentially suspicious or flagged listings.

4. To provide relevant property and locality information to support decision-making.

5. To provide rental assistance covering scam awareness, legal and tenancy rights, and government housing schemes.

6. To provide role-based workflows for customers, property owners, administrators and verification partners.

7. To improve transparency by displaying verification status and relevant verification information.

8. To establish a foundation for future recommendation, document-verification and fraud-detection capabilities.
