# Problem Statement

The current rental process is fragmented and trust-sensitive.

Renters often need to use multiple sources to discover properties, evaluate locality information, understand safety considerations, verify listing authenticity, identify potential scams, and obtain legal or housing guidance.

### Major Problems

1. **Fragmented Information**  
   Property listings, locality information, safety information and rental guidance are generally distributed across different sources.

2. **Verification Difficulty**  
   Renters may find it difficult to determine whether a listing and the person offering the property are legitimate.

3. **Scam Exposure**  
   Fake or duplicated listings, misleading information and requests for advance or token payments can create financial risk.

4. **Lack of Rental Guidance**  
   Information related to scam awareness, tenancy rights, documentation and government housing schemes may require separate research.

5. **Decision-Making Complexity**  
   Renters must combine information from several sources before deciding whether a property is suitable and trustworthy.

## Problem Definition

There is a need for a unified rental assistance platform that combines property discovery, structured verification, contextual information and rental guidance to support safer and more informed rental decisions.
