## Analysis

The comparison shows that existing platforms already provide many individual rental-related capabilities.

Rentify is not intended to claim that these capabilities are individually new. Its focus is their integration into a structured workflow:

**DISCOVER → VERIFY → UNDERSTAND → DECIDE**

The primary contribution is therefore integration, transparency and renter-centric decision support.
