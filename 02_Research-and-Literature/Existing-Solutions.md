# Existing Solutions

Rentify's existing-solution analysis examined major rental/property platforms and managed accommodation services.

## NoBroker

Focuses on direct owner-property discovery and reducing brokerage involvement.

## 99acres

Provides large-scale property discovery across residential and commercial categories and supports interactions among owners, brokers and developers.

## MagicBricks

Provides broad property inventory and search functionality across multiple locations and property categories.

## Housing.com

Provides property discovery together with locality information and listing-data verification practices.

## Managed Accommodation Platforms

Platforms such as Stanza Living, NestAway and Zolo focus on managed accommodation and related rental services.

## Observation

Existing platforms provide strong discovery capabilities and, in some cases, verification or managed-accommodation features. However, the reviewed solutions address different parts of the rental journey.

Rentify focuses on bringing discovery, verification status, contextual information and structured rental assistance together into a single renter-facing workflow.
