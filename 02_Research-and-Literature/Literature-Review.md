# Literature Review

The literature reviewed for Rentify was organized into four major areas: rental recommendation, fake-listing/fraud detection, property verification, and location-aware recommendation.

## 1. Rental Recommendation

Polohakul et al. (2021) investigated a hybrid session-based and content-based recommendation approach for solving the item cold-start problem in real-estate recommendation.

Nguyen et al. (2023) explored context-based recommendation using user profiles and browsing context for real-estate investment.

These studies demonstrate the usefulness of recommendation and contextual information for property discovery, but do not address the complete rental verification and assistance workflow.

## 2. Fake Listing and Fraud Detection

Mohd Amin et al. (2024) investigated clustering-based classification of fake real-estate listings using K-means and distance-based analysis.

Such approaches demonstrate that listing characteristics can be used to identify potentially suspicious properties. However, the reviewed work was evaluated on a static dataset and was not integrated into a live rental verification workflow.

## 3. Property Verification

Xue et al. (2021) proposed a blockchain-based housing rental system involving identity and property authentication and smart contracts.

Bhanushali et al. (2020) explored blockchain-based property ownership verification for reducing fraud in buying and selling transactions.

These approaches strengthen trust through technical verification mechanisms, but introduce additional infrastructure requirements and do not focus on the broader renter decision process.

## 4. Location-Aware Recommendation

Levandoski et al. (2012) introduced LARS, a location-aware recommender system using spatial information together with user and item ratings.

This provides a foundation for location-aware recommendation, although it is not specifically designed around rental amenities, safety or legal guidance.

## Overall Observation

The reviewed literature provides useful techniques for individual stages of the rental journey, but these areas are generally studied separately.
