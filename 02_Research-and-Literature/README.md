# 02 — Research and Literature

This section documents the academic research, existing rental platforms, comparative analysis and research gap identified during the development of Rentify.

## Contents

- `Literature-Review.md` — Review of relevant academic research
- `Existing-Solutions.md` — Analysis of existing rental and accommodation platforms
- `Comparative-Analysis.md` — Feature-level comparison of existing solutions and Rentify
- `Research-Gap.md` — Identified research and integration gap

## Research Areas

The literature review focuses on:

- Rental property recommendation
- Fake listing and fraud detection
- Property and ownership verification
- Location-aware recommendation

## Existing Solutions

The project reviews:

- NoBroker
- 99acres
- MagicBricks
- Housing.com
- Managed rental and co-living platforms

## Research Position

The reviewed literature and existing platforms address different stages of the rental journey.

Rentify's primary contribution is the **integration of property discovery, verification, contextual information and rental assistance into a unified renter-facing decision-support workflow**.

The project does not claim that the individual capabilities are completely new.
