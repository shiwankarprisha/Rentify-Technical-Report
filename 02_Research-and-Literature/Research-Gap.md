# Research Gap

## Identified Gap

The reviewed literature and existing platforms address different stages of the rental journey.

Research has separately explored:

- Property recommendation
- Fake-listing and fraud detection
- Property and ownership verification
- Location-aware recommendation

However, these capabilities are generally evaluated or implemented independently.

## Specific Gap

The reviewed research and commercial landscape did not demonstrate a unified renter-facing workflow combining:

1. Property discovery
2. Owner/property verification
3. Verification status transparency
4. Location and contextual information
5. Scam awareness
6. Legal and tenancy guidance
7. Government housing-scheme information

for the independent-owner rental context.

## Rentify's Contribution

Rentify addresses this gap through integration rather than by introducing a completely new algorithm.

The proposed platform combines:

**Property Discovery + Verification + Context + Rental Assistance**

within one decision-support workflow.

## Technical Position

The current prototype demonstrates the workflow using deterministic filtering/ranking, simulated verification states and structured assistance content.

Advanced machine-learning recommendation, AI-assisted document verification and external registry integrations are proposed future capabilities rather than current implementations.

## Research Gap Statement

> Existing research and platforms address individual stages of the rental journey, while Rentify proposes their integration into a transparent, renter-centric workflow for safer and more informed rental decision-making.
