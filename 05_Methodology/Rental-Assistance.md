# Rental Assistance Methodology

Rentify extends beyond property discovery by providing structured rental assistance to help customers understand common risks and responsibilities.

## Assistance Categories

### 1. Scam Awareness

Customers can learn about documented rental-scam patterns and warning signs.

Examples include:

- Unrealistic rental offers
- Upfront token-payment requests
- Fake verification charges
- Identity or listing inconsistencies

### 2. Legal and Tenancy Rights

The platform provides general information related to rental and tenancy considerations.

This information is intended for awareness and guidance and does not replace professional legal advice.

### 3. Government Housing Schemes

Rentify provides information about relevant housing-support initiatives and schemes, where applicable.

## Assistance Integration

Rental assistance is available:

- From the main Assistance Portal
- Within relevant property-detail workflows

This keeps guidance accessible while the renter is evaluating a property.

## Decision-Support Role

The assistance module supports the final **UNDERSTAND → DECIDE** stages of the Rentify workflow.

It helps customers consider risks, rights and available housing support alongside property information.
