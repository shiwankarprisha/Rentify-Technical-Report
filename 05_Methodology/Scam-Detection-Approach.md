# Scam Detection Approach

Rental scams can involve misleading listings, identity misuse, unrealistic prices and requests for upfront payments.

Rentify treats scam awareness and discrepancy identification as part of its trust-support workflow.

## Scam Patterns Considered

The platform provides awareness around patterns such as:

- Fake or reused property listings
- Unrealistically low rental prices
- Requests for advance/token payments
- Fake premium or verification charges
- Identity or ownership discrepancies
- Existing-tenant or unauthorized subletting situations

## Current Prototype

The prototype demonstrates scam and discrepancy awareness through:

- Flagged listing states
- Recorded discrepancy reasons
- Warning messages
- Scam-awareness guidance
- Verification workflow records

The current prototype does **not** implement a machine-learning scam classifier.

## Proposed Automated Detection

Future versions can use rule-based and machine-learning approaches to identify suspicious listing characteristics.

Potential signals include:

- Price anomalies
- Repeated listing information
- Suspicious textual patterns
- Duplicate images
- Unusual listing behaviour
- Identity/document inconsistencies

## Detection Principle

Automated detection should identify **potentially suspicious listings**, not declare with certainty that a person or listing is fraudulent.

Human review and appropriate verification remain important for high-risk cases.
