# Assistance Module

The Assistance Module provides structured rental guidance to support informed decision-making.

## Assistance Categories

The prototype provides three major categories.

### 1. Documented Scam Typologies

The module explains common rental scam patterns and warning signs.

Examples include:

- Unrealistic rental offers
- Upfront token-payment requests
- Fake verification or premium charges
- Identity or ownership discrepancies

### 2. Legal & Tenancy Rights

The module provides general information to help renters understand rental and tenancy considerations.

The information is intended for awareness and does not replace professional legal advice.

### 3. Government Housing Schemes

The module provides information related to relevant housing-support initiatives, including:

- ARHCs
- PMAY

## Integration with Property Details

The Assistance Module is also accessible from the property details interface through the:

**Decision Guidance Hub**

This allows customers to access guidance while evaluating a property.

## Full Assistance Portal

Customers can use:

**Open Full Assistance Portal**

to access the complete assistance interface.

## Implementation Status

The assistance content is currently implemented as structured frontend content.

The prototype does not dynamically retrieve legal or government information from external services.

Future versions can introduce verified and regularly updated external information sources.
