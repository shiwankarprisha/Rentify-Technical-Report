# Multilingual Support

Multilingual support is a proposed future enhancement for Rentify.

## Objective

Rental assistance and property information should be accessible to users with different language preferences.

## Possible Languages

The platform could initially support commonly used Indian languages based on the target deployment region.

For example:

- English
- Hindi
- Marathi

Additional languages can be introduced as the platform expands geographically.

## Possible Areas of Translation

Multilingual support could cover:

- Navigation
- Property information
- Rental assistance
- Scam-awareness guidance
- Legal and tenancy information
- Government scheme information
- User instructions

## Implementation Approach

A localization framework could maintain translated interface strings separately from application logic.

Future content-management functionality could also support translated assistance content.

## Considerations

Legal and government-related information should be translated carefully and reviewed for accuracy.

Machine translation should not be treated as sufficient for legally sensitive content without appropriate review.

## Current Status

The current prototype is primarily English-language and does not provide a complete multilingual interface.
