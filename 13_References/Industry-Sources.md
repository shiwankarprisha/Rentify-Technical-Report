# Industry Sources

This section documents existing rental platforms reviewed during the analysis of Rentify.

## Platforms Reviewed

### NoBroker
Reviewed for owner-listed properties, rental discovery, and brokerage-reduction features.

### 99acres
Reviewed for large-scale property discovery and its broker/developer/owner ecosystem.

### MagicBricks
Reviewed as a major property discovery and listing platform.

### Housing.com
Reviewed for property discovery and listing-data verification practices.

### Managed Rental / Co-living Platforms
Platforms such as Stanza Living, NestAway, and Zolo were reviewed to understand managed accommodation and rental-service models.

## Relevance to Rentify

The comparative analysis indicates that existing platforms provide strong property discovery capabilities, while Rentify focuses on integrating discovery with an explicit verification workflow, contextual rental information, and renter-oriented decision guidance.
